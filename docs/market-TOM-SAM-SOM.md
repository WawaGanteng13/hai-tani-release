# Hai Tani (TaniAI) Market: TOM / SAM / SOM

## 1. TOM — Total Obtainable Market (agritech spend)

- Primary source: Indonesian agritech market USD 885.70M (2023), 15.6% CAGR, projected USD 3,034.9M (2033); drivers: government support & food security — https://www.kdmarketinsights.com/reports/indonesia-agritech-market/7611
- Cross-check: USD 500M (2023), growing 15.6% (2024-2033) — https://startuprise.org/agritech-startups-of-indonesia-2024/
- Most relevant sub-segment (AI agents): AI in Indonesian agriculture USD 142M (2025) → USD 786M (2031), 33.2% CAGR — https://mobilityforesights.com/product/indonesia-artificial-intelligence-in-agriculture-market
- Math (author's rate assumption):
  - 2023 TOM = 885.7M × Rp15,800 ≈ Rp14.0T; cross-check 500M ≈ Rp7.9T → TOM range Rp7.9-14.0T
  - 2033 projection = 3,034.9M × Rp15,800 ≈ Rp48.0T
  - AI-agriculture 2025 = 142M × Rp15,800 ≈ Rp2.2T

## 2. SAM — Serviceable Available Market (WA-reachable farmers)

- Population basis: 29.34M farm businesses (2023) vs 31.70M (2013); ~40% of farmers >55 yrs; millennials 21% — https://journal.sragenkab.go.id/index.php/sukowati/article/view/493
- Connectivity basis: 221,563,479 internet users / 79.5% penetration; rural 30.5% — https://apjii.or.id/berita/d/apjii-jumlah-pengguna-internet-indonesia-tembus-221-juta-orang ; early-2024 cross-check: 185.3M internet users (66.5%), 353.3M mobile connections (126.8%) — https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2024-indonesia?rq=Indonesia
- WA basis: #1 platform, ~90-90.8% of internet users/month — https://www.ungkit.com/2026/10/the-most-widely-used-social-media.html
- Bottom-up math (upper bound, optimistic as it applies national penetration to rural):
  - 29.34M × 79.5% × 90% ≈ 21.0M WA-reachable farmers
  - Author-assumed ARPU Rp100-300K/year (price-info + crop-clinic subscription; no willingness-to-pay source exists)
  - SAM ≈ 21M × Rp100-300K ≈ Rp2.1-6.3T/year
