# Hai Tani — Demo Video Script (draft, ~3 min)

Rule: OPEN and CLOSE with negotiation. Q&A/photo get 10–15 s max as agent tools.
App UI is Indonesian; VO can be Indonesian with English subtitles (or English VO).

## Shot 0 — Hook (0:00–0:15)
- Screen: farmer phone, WhatsApp chat with Hai Tani.
- VO: "Petani Indonesia menjual lewat tengkulak yang itu-itu saja. Hai Tani mempertemukan buyer langsung dengan petani — lewat WhatsApp."
- Show: buyer message "butuh agave 5 kg" → bot replies with offer + farmer list.

## Shot 1 — Buyer demand with spec (0:15–0:50)
- Buyer: "butuh agave 5 kg spek: daun lebar".
- Bot creates OFR-xxx, shows spec captured.
- Cut to farmer phone: receives offer + price ask + spec verification question.
- VO: "Setiap permintaan tercatat; spesifikasi buyer ikut diteruskan."

## Shot 2 — Farmer verification (0:50–1:20)
- Farmer: "jual agave 50000 per kg" → bot asks "sanggup penuhi spek?"
- Farmer: "sanggup" → allocation recorded; show second farmer answering "tidak" → excluded line.
- VO: "Petani verifikasi spek sendiri. Yang tidak sanggup, keluar dari alokasi."

## Shot 3 — Budget match + deal (1:20–1:50)
- Buyer: "budget 60000" → COCOK → "deal oke".
- Show receipt: deal recorded, tiered commission (e.g. TUMBUH 2%), cash entry.
- VO: "Budget cocok, deal tercatat, komisi transparan sesuai tier."

## Shot 4 — Dashboard (1:50–2:20)
- Admin dashboard: deal appears in Kemitraan tab, map pin, price anomaly alert.
- VO: "Semua terpantau: kemitraan, peta, harga pasar, peringatan anomali."

## Shot 5 — Agent tools, fast (2:20–2:35, MAX 15 s)
- Quick cuts: crop photo → diagnosis + [REF-xxx] match + saved case; fertilizer question → cited answer.
- VO (one line): "Didukung asisten agronomi dengan referensi terverifikasi."

## Shot 6 — Close (2:35–3:00)
- Back to WhatsApp: buyer "deal oke", farmer confirmation.
- End card: "Hai Tani — Dari Petani, untuk Kesejahteraan Petani."
- VO: "Dari petani, untuk kesejahteraan petani."

## Recording notes
- Use two test phones/numbers (buyer + farmer) + screen capture of dashboard.
- Quiet traffic 10 min before recording (anti-spam same-text window).
- Do NOT show: admin passwords, tokens, private emails, real farmer PII.
