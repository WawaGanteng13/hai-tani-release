## antislop

Anti Slop: Rules for AI Coding Agents (https://github.com/miqdadbadjuber/anti-slop).
Filter out generic AI slop, clichés, ungrounded metrics, fake testimonials, and decorative visual clutter.
For UI, copy, accessibility, responsive mobile layout, and code comments, follow `antislop.md` and the task-specific skills:

- **Core Filter & Hard Gates:** `antislop.md`
- **UI / Visual Craft:** `skills/antislop-ui/SKILL.md`
- **Copywriting & Voice:** `skills/antislop-copywriting/SKILL.md`
- **Accessibility & Human Factors:** `skills/antislop-human/SKILL.md`
- **Mobile & Responsive Layouts:** `skills/antislop-layoutmobile/SKILL.md`
- **Code Comments Quality:** `skills/antislop-code/SKILL.md`

### Core Principles
1. **Filter, Not Style Guide:** Prohibit AI clichés (no random glowing gradients, fake statistics, floating cards inside cards, empty hype words like "supercharge" or "revolutionize") without stifling genuine product identity.
2. **Honest Copy & Grounded Data:** Speak with professional composure, concrete facts, and authentic domain language.
3. **Intentional Hierarchy:** Ensure mathematical padding, clear typographic scale, and accessible WCAG AA contrast.
